# Changelog

## 0.2.1

- Faster search, which always completes the first two depths.
- Better choice between equally good moves.
- Renamed and simplified settings.

## 0.2.0

- Iterative deepening: the engine searches as deep as the available time allows, instead of searching at a fixed depth.
- Mates are evaluated correctly.
- Fixes in move selection, in the UCI protocol and more robust output.
